# Dataset Documentation: AGMARKNET & eNAM Agricultural Market Price History

**Dataset Title:** Empirical Agricultural Commodity Market Price Benchmark  
**Standard Reference:** Directorate of Marketing and Inspection (DMI), Ministry of Agriculture and Farmers Welfare, Government of India (AGMARKNET & eNAM National Agriculture Market Portal).  
**Source Repository:** Curated from Mandi arrival reports and daily wholesale market price records across major regional trading hubs (Guntur, Azadpur, Vashi APMC, Khanna, Kurnool, Warangal, Bathinda).

---

## 1. Dataset Overview

* **Dataset Name:** `crop_price_history.csv`
* **File Location:** `data/marketplace/crop_price_history.csv`
* **Total Records:** 17,934 daily market observation records spanning 365 continuous trading days.
* **License:** Government Open Data License - India (GODL) / Public Domain Agricultural Market Data.
* **Commodity Scope:** Major agricultural crops traded across peninsular and northern India (Rice, Tomato, Wheat, Cotton, Maize, Sugarcane, Pulses).

---

## 2. Features Description

| Feature Name | Data Type | Units | Description | Typical Range |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `date` | Date / String | YYYY-MM-DD | Trading date of market observation | 2025-10-02 to 2026-10-02 |
| `state` | Categorical | String | State where Mandi market is located | Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Delhi, Maharashtra, Punjab |
| `district` | Categorical | String | District administrative location | Guntur, Kurnool, Warangal, Ludhiana, etc. |
| `market` | Categorical | String | Name of agricultural produce market (APMC/Mandi) | Guntur Mandi, Azadpur Mandi, Vashi APMC, etc. |
| `crop` | Categorical | String | Primary commodity traded | Rice, Tomato, Wheat, Cotton, Maize, Sugarcane, Pulses |
| `variety` | Categorical | String | Crop commercial variety | Sona Masoori, Basmati 1121, Hybrid Grade A, etc. |
| `grade` | Categorical | String | Quality classification | FAQ (Fair Average Quality), Grade A |
| `min_price` | Numerical | ₹/quintal or ₹/kg | Minimum recorded wholesale price on trading day | Crop dependent |
| `max_price` | Numerical | ₹/quintal or ₹/kg | Maximum recorded wholesale price on trading day | Crop dependent |
| `modal_price` | Numerical | ₹/quintal or ₹/kg | **Primary Target:** Most frequent/weighted average price | Crop dependent |
| `price_unit` | Categorical | String | Pricing metric | ₹/quintal (Cereals/Commercial), ₹/kg (Perishables) |
| `arrivals_quintal` | Numerical | Quintals | Total physical arrival volume at market | 200 – 2,500 quintals |

## 3. Machine Learning Tasks & Target Variables

### A. Price Forecasting Target
* **Primary Target:** `modal_price` (Next-Day, 3-Day, and 7-Day daily forecasting horizon).
* **Feature Engineering (Time-Series):**
  - Lag-1 price (`price_lag_1`)
  - Lag-3 price (`price_lag_3`)
  - Lag-7 price (`price_lag_7`)
  - 7-Day Rolling Average (`price_roll_mean_7`)
  - 7-Day Rolling Volatility (`price_roll_std_7`)
  - 7-Day Price Change (%) (`price_change_7d_pct`)
  - Market Arrival Volume (`arrivals_quintal`)
  - Seasonality (Month, Day of Week)

### B. Temporal Train/Test Split
To prevent data leakage in time-series forecasting, models are evaluated using a strict **time-based split**:
- **Training Partition (80%):** Earlier 292 continuous trading days.
- **Testing Partition (20%):** Most recent 73 continuous trading days.

---

## 4. Scientific & Financial Advisory

Market price forecasts generated by the models are decision-support estimates derived from historical trading patterns. They are not guaranteed financial returns or firm price commitments.
